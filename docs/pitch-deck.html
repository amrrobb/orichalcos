02 / 08 · The problem

The solution

Three roles, one protocol.

A risk-management primitive for AI trading agents — built on 0G for verifiability, Hyperliquid for execution.

I · Trader
Mints a Strategy Agent INFT. Bonds USDC. Runs an AI strategy sealed inside 0G Compute TEE. Earns on success — bond fully slashed on breach.
II · Allocator
Browses Strategy Agents by verified P&L. Pays a premium for protected exposure. Receives a claim payout if the strategy breaches its drawdown threshold.
III · LP
Deposits USDC into the protocol pool. Earns premium yield. On breach, also receives the unallocated bond residual. Zero principal risk in v3.

03 / 08 · The solution

How it works

Four primitives, one verifiable trail.

Every trade leaves a four-piece attestation that anyone can verify, but no one can read the strategy itself.

I · SEAL

0G Storage

Strategy encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Merkle root on chain. Operator never sees plaintext.
II · DECIDE

0G Compute TEE

Decryption + inference inside Intel TDX + H100. Every call returns a signed chatId.
III · EXECUTE

Hyperliquid

Real perpetual DEX. Real testnet order IDs. Real fills, real slippage, real proof.
IV · ATTEST

0G Chain

Every trade's chatId + storage root + HL oid + P&L delta committed on chain, atomically.

05 / 08 · Live on chain

Business model

Premiums are split three ways.

On every policy purchased, premium routes through a Nexus-Mutual-style three-way split that aligns all three sides of the protocol.

70%
Pool LPs
20%
Trader
10%
Treasury
  • 70% to Pool LPs. Underwriting capacity provider. Earns premium yield + slashed-bond residuals. Currently zero principal risk (bond ≥ max claim enforced at policy issue).
  • 20% to Trader — held in escrow, forfeited on breach. Real performance fee, not a capture-on-buy fee. Trader earns it only if the epoch settles cleanly, aligning the bond-as-reputation-signal with bond-as-yield-source. Roadmap to v3.1.
  • 10% to Protocol Reserve. Funds dev grants and (v3.1) insurance backstop when LP underwriting becomes real. Governance-controlled allocation deferred to v3.2.

Additional revenue levers: 50 USDC mint fee per Strategy Agent · 1% settlement fee on claim payouts · 2.5% royalty on Strategy INFT secondary market trades. Premium BPS itself (currently 12.5%) is a governance lever in v3.2.
