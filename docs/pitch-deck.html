01 / 08 · Title

The dilemma

Every AI strategy sits on one of two horns.

If you reveal it, copy-traders erode the edge. If you hide it, no allocator can trust it. There has been no primitive for verifiable performance without revealed alpha — until now.

Reveal the strategy

Alpha decays on contact.

Open-source bots get front-run. Discord alpha is dead before retail can copy it. Edge that's legible is edge that's gone.

VS
Hide the strategy

Allocators can't tell rug from real edge.

Anon vaults rug. "Trust me bro" P&L screenshots can't be audited. Capital can't price a black-box claim.

02 / 08 · The dilemma

What we built

A promise-keeping market.

Three primitives. One verifiable trail. Zero leaked alpha.

I · Bond
A Strategy Agent posts a USDC bond against a drawdown promise. The strategy itself runs sealed inside 0G TEE. The bond is what's at stake.
II · Policy
An allocator pays a premium to buy a policy. If the agent breaks its drawdown promise, the bond pays the allocator. If it doesn't, the premium splits to the trader and LPs.
III · Settlement
Anyone can call markBreach() or settleEpoch() on chain. No oracle. No multisig. The bond, the trades, and the threshold all sit in the same contract.

03 / 08 · What we built

Tech stack

Every dependency is load-bearing.

Pull any of these four out and the protocol stops being interesting. None of them is decoration.

I · Seal

0G Compute TEE

Strategy weights stay encrypted at rest, decrypt only inside Intel TDX + H100. Every inference returns a signed chatId.
— without it, alpha leaks the moment a node is compromised
II · Store

0G Storage

The full reasoning bundle for each trade — prompt, response, signature — is content-addressed and pinned. The on-chain attestation carries only the merkle root.
— without it, "TEE-signed" is unverifiable post-hoc
III · Execute

Hyperliquid testnet

Trades fill on a real perpetual DEX with real slippage, real liquidity, real testnet order IDs that any judge can click and verify.
— without it, the track record is just a database row
IV · Settle

0G Chain

Bond escrow, policy registry, breach threshold, and permissionless settlement all live on Galileo. Composable with the rest of 0G.
— without it, no fair, permissionless payout

The mechanism

From bond to settlement.

A single epoch traces a clean path through the contract — no oracles, no off-chain coordinators, no special privileges.

01

Trader bonds

USDC posted, drawdown threshold set, epoch opens.
02

Strategy runs in TEE

Sealed inference signs each trade decision.
03

Hyperliquid fills

Real testnet orders, attested on 0G Chain.
04

Allocator buys policy

Premium goes into the pool. Bond is reserved as their max claim.
05

Breach or expiry

Equity ≤ threshold ⇒ markBreach(). Otherwise epoch settles cleanly.
06

Permissionless payout

Any wallet can call settleEpoch(). Funds route by the contract.

Why this fits Track 2

Agentic Trading Arena, on its own terms.

The track description names three things. We address each directly, with the same vocabulary.

AI-driven perpetual strategy agents

Each Strategy Agent INFT is a self-contained, sealed AI trader running real perpetual fills on Hyperliquid. Bond + drawdown promise gives it economic identity, not just a token id.

Sealed Inference & TEE-based execution

Weights live encrypted on 0G Storage. Decryption + inference happen only inside 0G Compute TEE. The protocol never sees the strategy; the strategy never leaks.

Front-running mitigation

There's nothing to front-run: signals never leave the enclave, only signed fills. And because the bond is at stake, even a leaked agent has no incentive to telegraph itself.

Verifiable finance, framed as a market: traders bond their reputation as collateral, allocators price that reputation, and the protocol enforces the promise.

07 / 08 · Track 2 fit

What's next

v3 ships the primitive. v4 enriches the promise.

A drawdown cap is the minimum honest promise. The mechanism scales to richer ones — and to a first-class AI trader-of-traders.

Variable premium

Premium BPS scales with the agent's verified track record on Hyperliquid. A first-epoch agent pays one rate; a 10-epoch survivor pays another. Priced, not negotiated.

Richer promises

minTrades + minPnL guards on top of drawdown. A trader can promise "at least N fills, at least X% net, drawdown ≤ Y%" — and allocators get a multi-dimensional contract to underwrite.

AI-agent allocator

The natural next role: an AI that buys policies across Strategy Agents the way a fund-of-funds buys exposure. Sealed reasoning, on-chain portfolio. Track 2's vision, two layers deep.
