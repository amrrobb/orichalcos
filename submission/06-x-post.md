## Common pitfalls to avoid

- **Don't omit any tag.** All four `@0G_labs @0g_CN @0g_Eco @HackQuest_` are required. Easy to miss `@HackQuest_` (trailing underscore).
- **Don't post without a visual.** "Slide-only or concept-only" warnings in the demo-video rules suggest the org expects visual proof. A text-only post may be flagged.
- **Don't link to a private demo URL.** If `orichalcos.vercel.app` is referenced in the post, make sure that URL still resolves at the moment a judge opens it.
- **Verify the post is public**, not protected. Test by opening in an incognito window.
